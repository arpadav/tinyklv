# Intermediate

Five chapters for when you are comfortable with basic roundtrips and ready
to tackle real-world framing concerns: user-written codecs, sentinel
resync, nesting, BER keys, and defaults.

- [06 - Custom Encoder/Decoder](./06-custom-encoder-decoder.md) - write your own codec functions.
- [07 - Sentinel Seeking](./07-sentinel-seeking.md) - recover framing over noisy streams.
- [08 - Nested Packets](./08-nested-packets.md) - compose packets from sub-packets.
- [09 - BER-Keyed Containers](./09-ber-keyed.md) - multi-byte OID keys.
- [10 - Defaults and Init](./10-defaults-and-init.md) - container and field defaults.
